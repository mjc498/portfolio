Interview Reflection

What responses came naturally?

The responses that came naturally were the ones about myself and my past experiences. It was easy to reference numbers and skills pulled directly from my résumé because I know exactly what I contributed. Although I’m confident in what I did, I think it would also help employers to see more of my personality beyond my résumé. In future interviews, I want to share more about what motivates me and how I work with others.

My best responses:

  • Tell me about yourself: I am a senior UX/UI student at Drexel University in the 5-year, 3-co-op program. I have 24 months of full-time corporate experience spanning web development, digital marketing, and project management. I am seeking opportunities in UX/UI design, product design, or entry-level product management, where I can combine user research, interaction design, technical knowledge, and cross-functional collaboration to help shape useful, accessible digital products.
  • What are your strengths and weaknesses: My strengths are attention to detail and using data to make decisions. At NBME, I raised accessibility from 32% to 99%, and at SEPTA I boosted engagement by 25%. My weakness is that I can ramble when explaining my work, so I keep a list of examples organized as situation, task, action, and result, and I practice leading with the main point.

What responses did you struggle with?

I struggled with my STAR responses. I’ve worked four co-ops, so it’s hard for me to choose a specific situation in the moment. After hearing that my classmates keep a journal or list of questions that require a STAR response, I felt inspired to start my own, so I don’t feel lost when answering them. I plan to write down a few situations from each co-op ahead of time so I can choose one quickly.

Questions I struggled to answer:

  • Describe a time when you failed at something. What did you learn?
  • Describe a challenge you faced and how you overcame it.

What type of feedback did you receive?

I received generally good feedback. My behavioral answers following the STAR format could have been better. It took me a while to formulate my responses, and I lacked confidence when explaining the situation. I left out some details because I feared they would bore the employer.

Feedback I received:

  • Strong opener: clear, under 90 seconds
  • Eye contact and open, engaged posture
  • Concise and direct, no rambling
  • Specific examples, with numbers where possible
  • Connected answers back to the role
  • Stayed calm on a tough question
  • Asked thoughtful questions at the end

Ideas for improving your answers and techniques?

I will keep a handy list of general questions and answers ready to practice, especially behavioral questions. I also need to stop rambling and get straight to the point without being too vague.
