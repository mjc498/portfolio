NBME

driving digital accessibility and marketing excellence at a healthcare assessment leader

The Overview

Introduction

During my six-month co-op at the NBME, I worked as a Digital Marketing Analyst, bridging the gap between design, accessibility, and digital marketing. NBME is a nonprofit organization that creates high-quality assessments for medical professionals and executives in the medical education sectors.

My role focused on enhancing digital accessibility, optimizing marketing campaigns, and streamlining content workflows. I tackled projects ranging from remediating hundreds of PDFs to building web components using agile methodologies and Figma, all while ensuring NBME's digital presence remained inclusive and effective.

Context and Challenge

Background

NBME's digital ecosystem includes a complex website, extensive documentation, and marketing campaigns that reach healthcare professionals worldwide. When I joined the team, there was a pressing need to improve accessibility compliance and optimize digital marketing performance.

The Problem

Over 700 PDFs needed remediation to meet WCAG guidelines, the website required comprehensive accessibility audits, and there was room to improve content management workflows. The challenge was to address these issues systematically while maintaining day-to-day operations.

Goals & Objectives

My primary objectives were to ensure NBME's digital content was accessible to all users, optimize marketing campaigns for better engagement, and streamline content workflows across teams. I needed to balance technical execution with strategic thinking, all while learning new tools and adapting to a professional environment.

Key Projects & Solutions

PDF Accessibility Remediation

I led the remediation of over 700 inaccessible PDFs, transforming them into WCAG-compliant documents. Using Axe DevTools to identify barriers and Adobe Acrobat to fix them, I created a systematic workflow that ensured consistency. Each PDF required careful attention: adding alt text to images, establishing proper heading structures, ensuring reading order, and tagging tables correctly.

Every remediated PDF meant that screen reader users, people with cognitive disabilities, and those using assistive technologies could access critical medical education resources. The scale of the project taught me the importance of creating repeatable processes and documenting best practices for future teams.

Website Accessibility Auditing

I conducted comprehensive accessibility audits of NBME's website, identifying barriers that prevented users from fully engaging with content. I manually verified color contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, focus indicators, and screen reader compatibility.

Each audit resulted in detailed reports with prioritized recommendations. I collaborated with others to resolve issues, explaining why certain fixes mattered and how they impacted real users. This experience deepened my understanding of WCAG guidelines and reinforced that accessibility is fundamental to good design.

Digital Marketing Analytics

I analyzed campaign performance using Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, and Looker Studio. By examining metrics like click-through rates, bounce rates, and conversion paths, I identified opportunities to optimize engagement and recommended strategic decisions.

This work showed me how data-driven decision-making enhances user experiences. Understanding what resonates with audiences and why helped me think more holistically about digital design.

The Results

Conclusion

Over six months, I remediated 700+ PDFs to WCAG compliance, conducted comprehensive website accessibility audits, and built multiple web components through agile sprints. My marketing analytics work led to data-driven optimizations that improved campaign engagement, and my workflow improvements increased cross-team efficiency.

Beyond the numbers, this experience transformed how I approach design. I now see accessibility as a creative challenge that makes products better for everyone. I learned to work within complex organizational structures, communicate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders, and balance multiple priorities without compromising quality.

Most importantly, I learned the value of persistence. Remediating 700+ PDFs wasn't glamorous, but knowing that each one opened access to someone who previously couldn't engage with the content made every hour worthwhile. This experience reinforced that good design is about creating experiences that work for everyone, regardless of ability.
